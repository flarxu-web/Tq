Crowd Sourcing Platform

Public Transport Experience Intelligence

Collect, analyse, and act on rider feedback in real time. This dashboard converts community submissions into a unified view of satisfaction, accessibility, and operational performance across the network.

Project Milestones

  1. Plan

    Defined business objectives and translated them into wireframes for the dashboard experience.

  2. Build

    Delivered the AI models and automated data pipeline that transform raw feedback into structured insight.

  3. Test

    Completed SIT for data flows and visuals, followed by UAT to validate the solution with business stakeholders.

  4. Deploy

    Launched the platform, provided production handover, and equipped the client with supporting documentation.
